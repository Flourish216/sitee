Project Constellation

I pictured every sketch as a little satellite. They drift, collide, and occasionally respond to passing gravity (your cursor). Click a wandering light to jump to its story below.

drag / hover / click the satellites

Satellite 01 · Vox Mechanica

A babbling geometry robot practices machine poetry. Click inside the frame to wake it up—its voice turns into chirps, and it streams glitch text as it improvises.

Satellite 02 · Candy Bloom

A strange bear made of sugar vapor. Feed it little gummies; when it gets too full, it bursts into candy dust. Clear the pieces to let another tiny bear grow.

Satellite 03 · Chromatic Carpet

A hovering rug that ripples with color when you steer it. Move the cursor to tilt the loom and watch the gradients shift in mid-air.
