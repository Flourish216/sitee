Narrative Photograph

This photograph captures my younger brother at a moment of quiet curiosity. We share the same birthday, eighteen years apart, which makes this image a reflection on time, distance, and beginnings. His gaze extends beyond the frame, suggesting an early awareness of the world and an instinct to explore what lies ahead. I composed the image using negative space to emphasize uncertainty and possibility, allowing the background to remain soft and undefined. The lighting was simple and natural. Light from one side functions as the key light, shaping his face, while ambient back light softens the shadows. The darker background helps separate the subject and direct attention to his expression.
